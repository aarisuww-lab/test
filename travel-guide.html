Suggested routes

North loop, 7 days

Hanoi, Ninh Binh, Cao Bang, Ha Giang: karst, waterfalls and mountain passes.

Central heritage, 6 days

Hue, Hoi An, My Son, Quang Binh: citadels, old towns and caves.

Coastal calm, 5 days

Phu Yen and the nearby coast: slow travel, seafood and sunrise points.

Travel well

Practical

Check seasonal weather (the north is cool in winter, the centre rainy in autumn), book mountain transport early and carry some cash for villages.

Protect nature

Carry out your trash, avoid single-use plastic, stay on trails and keep your distance from wildlife and cave formations.

Respect culture

Dress modestly at temples, remove shoes where asked, ask before taking photos and learn a few Vietnamese greetings.
